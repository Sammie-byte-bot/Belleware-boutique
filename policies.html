01

Returns & refunds

If an item is not right, contact us promptly. The rules below explain the return window, what to include in a request and how refunds are handled.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Inspect your order as soon as it arrives

Please check your items at the pickup point before leaving, or as soon as possible after a delivery. If an item is missing, incorrect, damaged or defective, let the pickup attendant know where possible and contact Bellewear within 48 hours of collection or delivery. Please keep the packaging until we have advised you on the next steps.

When an item can be returned

  • Wrong, damaged or defective item: tell us within 48 hours of receiving it. Include your order number, a description of the issue and clear photos where possible. We will review the report and arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.
  • Change of mind for an in-stock item: contact us within 7 days of collection or delivery. The item must be unworn, unwashed and unused, with its tags attached and original packaging included.
  • Items marked “Ready in X days”: these are ordered from our supplier after purchase and cannot be returned for change of mind. This does not remove our responsibility to address an item that is wrong, damaged or defective.

How to request a return

Contact us before sending or handing back an item so we can confirm the next steps. Send your order number (which starts with ORD-), the item name, the reason for the request and photos if the item is damaged or incorrect. Please do not send an item to an address or pickup point until our team has confirmed where it should go.

  1. Email us or message/call the number below within the applicable return window.
  2. Wait for our team to review the request and provide return instructions.
  3. Keep the item, tags and packaging together while the request is reviewed.

Refunds and delivery charges

Once an approved return has been received and inspected, Bellewear will contact you to arrange an eligible refund within 7 working days. We will let you know if we need more information to process the refund.

Delivery fees are refunded when the return is due to an error on our part. For a change-of-mind return, delivery fees are not refunded.

Order cancellations

You can cancel an order from your account while its status is still Placed. Once we have ordered an item from our supplier, that order can no longer be cancelled. If you cannot access your account, contact us as soon as possible and include your order number.

Orders not collected

Orders remain at the pickup point for 7 days. If you need more time, contact us before the collection period ends. After 7 days, an uncollected order may be cancelled and refunded less any costs we cannot recover.

The return steps above do not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection law.

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Bellewear Boutique handles when you browse, create an account, place an order or contact us.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Who is responsible for your information

Bellewear Boutique, operated by codebee productions LTD, is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests, email admin@bellewear.com.

Information we collect

  • Account and contact details: information such as your name, email address and phone number that you provide when signing in or contacting us.
  • Order and fulfilment details: the items you order, order history, delivery or pickup details, and information needed to prepare and hand over your order.
  • Customer communications: messages and information you choose to share when asking for help, requesting a return or submitting a review.
  • Payment status: whether an order is awaiting payment or has been paid for at delivery or collection.
  • Browser storage: information such as your cart, wishlist and recently viewed items stored in your browser to make the shop work as expected.

Orders are paid for in person when delivered or collected. Bellewear does not request or store card numbers, online payment credentials or M-Pesa PINs.

How we use information

We use relevant information to:

  • create and manage your account and provide access to the shop;
  • process orders and coordinate delivery or pickup;
  • send order, collection and service updates;
  • answer questions, review return requests and resolve order issues;
  • protect the shop, help prevent fraud and maintain reliable services; and
  • meet applicable record-keeping and legal obligations.

Who may receive information

We share information only as needed to operate the shop and fulfil your request. This may include:

  • Google Firebase for the website's database and sign-in services.
  • Suppliers and pickup-point partners who need limited order details, such as your name, order number or phone number, to prepare or hand over an order.

We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser storage for shopping features such as your cart, wishlist and recently viewed items. You can clear this data in your browser settings; doing so may remove saved shopping information or affect how parts of the site work.

Keeping information safe and for how long

We use service providers and reasonable safeguards to support the operation of the shop. No website or online transfer can be guaranteed completely secure. We keep order information for as long as reasonably needed for fulfilment, customer support, accounting and legal obligations, then delete or retain it as required.

Your choices and data requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or raise an objection about how it is used. You may also ask us to close your account. Some information may need to be retained where required for a transaction, legal obligation or legitimate operational reason. Email admin@bellewear.com with your request; we may need to verify your identity before acting.

This policy is intended to reflect the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Please contact us if you have a concern about your personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this notice when our services or practices change. The “Last updated” date at the start of this section identifies the current version. Please check this page for the latest information.

Terms & conditions

These terms apply when you browse or place an order through the Bellewear Boutique website.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Using the website and your account

By using the site or placing an order, you agree to these terms. Please provide accurate contact, delivery and account information and keep your sign-in details private. Contact us if you believe someone else has accessed your account.

Do not use the site in a way that disrupts its operation, interferes with other customers or is unlawful. We may restrict access where needed to protect customers, the site or our services.

Products, availability and prices

Product information and images are provided to help you choose. Colours may look different on different screens. Product availability can change, and an item marked “Ready in X days” is ordered from a supplier after purchase; the stated timeframe is an estimate.

Prices are shown in Kenya Shillings (Ksh). If an item is unavailable or we discover a pricing or stock error before fulfilment, we may contact you to discuss the issue or cancel the affected order. If we cancel it, we will refund the amount paid for that order.

Orders and payment

An order is confirmed when you receive an order number. Please review your items and contact details before submitting the order. Payment Payment is collected in person: on delivery for delivery orders, or at the store when you collect a pickup order. No online payment is required when placing an order.

Do not send payment card numbers, account credentials or M-Pesa PINs to Bellewear by email, phone or chat.

Delivery and pickup

Choose from the delivery and pickup options shown at checkout. Home delivery is available only where offered. We will notify you when your order is ready for collection. To collect an order, you may be asked to provide your order number or pickup code and identification.

Delivery dates, pickup readiness and “Ready in X days” timings are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Please contact us if you have not received an update or need help with a collection.

Returns and cancellations

Our Returns & refunds policy explains return eligibility, time limits, cancellation rules and refund processing. Your applicable statutory consumer rights are not excluded by these terms.

Website content and service availability

We work to keep product information and the website available and accurate, but occasional errors, interruptions or maintenance may occur. We may update site content or features as our shop changes. If information affecting your order needs correction, we will take reasonable steps to contact you.

Responsibility and applicable law

Nothing in these terms removes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable rights under Kenyan consumer law. To the extent permitted by law, Bellewear is not responsible for indirect losses or delays caused by circumstances outside our reasonable control.

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya.
